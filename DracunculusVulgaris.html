About Dracunculus Vulgaris
You know, every part of this plant is dangerous; if ingested, it can be poisonous. Even touching this poisonous flower can cause skin irritation or allergic reactions. It has the smell of rotting meat for one day. The side effects are Severe Irritation: Ingestion causes immediate burning, pain, and swelling of the mouth, throat, and tongue. Digestive Distress: Symptoms include nausea, vomiting, stomach pain, and diarrhea. Respiratory Issues: Severe swelling can obstruct breathing. Neurological/Severe Effects: Ingestion may lead to serious complications such as dilated pupils, seizures, and, in extreme cases, death.
