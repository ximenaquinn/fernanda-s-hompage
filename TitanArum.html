About Titan Arum
While it only blooms for a brief period of three days, the Titan Arum releases a fleshy smell that can be incredibly toxic and overpowering. Despite its beauty, the Titan Arum is a toxic flower, and its poisonous nature is not limited to just its scent. The central spike of the plant contains poison, and touching it can result in severe irritation or even death. The side effects are Ingestion (Highly Unsafe): Causes intense burning, swelling of the mouth, tongue, and throat. It can lead to severe vomiting, diarrhea, and potential gastrointestinal bleeding. Skin/Eye Contact: The sap contains needle-like crystals (calcium oxalate) that cause skin irritation, dermatitis, or severe irritation if it contacts the eyes.
