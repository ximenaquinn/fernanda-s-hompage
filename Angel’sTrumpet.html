About Angels Trumpet
Angels Trumpet is undeniably one of the most dangerous flowers in the world. These pastel pink flowers may look alluring, but they can cause dangerous side effects, also known as the zombie effect. If you eat them, you might experience the incapability of retaining new memories that leave you helpless. This poisonous flower can lead to different types of hallucinations, finally resulting in death. These flowers look so dreamy in different shades like Yellow, Pink, White, Orange, Gold, and Peach. It is known to be one of the most beautiful poisonous flowers all over the world. The side effects are: Ingestion leads to severe anticholinergic toxicity, with symptoms appearing 1–4 hours after consumption and lasting up to several days.
