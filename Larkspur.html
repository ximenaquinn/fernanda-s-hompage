About Larkspur
Larkspur can cause weakness, drooling, abdominal pain, paralysis, and death in humans if injected. This poisonous flower may look captivating, but it has major side effects. Not just flowers, but the entire plant can cause death. Even brief contact with the larkspur flower can cause skin irritation or an allergic reaction. The side effects are: The alkaloids in the plant cause neuromuscular paralysis; clinical effects include constipation, colic, increased salivation, muscle tremors, stiffness, weakness, recumbency, and convulsions. Cardiac failure may occur, as can death from respiratory paralysis.
