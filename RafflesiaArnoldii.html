About Rafflesia Arnoldii
It is the largest flower in the world. It weighs fifteen pounds. It’s like a parasitic monster flower with no roots or stem, blooming for only 5 days and releasing the smell of rotten flesh. Moreover, its blooming period lasts merely five days, during which it emits a putrid smell akin to rotten flesh, making it one of the most infamous and poisonous flowers for humans. The side effects are Skin Irritation: Touching the Rafflesia plant can cause skin irritation and allergic reactions. Toxicity/Poisoning: All parts of the plant are considered poisonous. Ingestion, although rare, has been associated with side effects such as nausea and severe abdominal pain. Severe Symptoms: In high doses, contact or ingestion of similar poisonous flowers can lead to more severe reactions like vomiting, diarrhea, and muscle pain.
