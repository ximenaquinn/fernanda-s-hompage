About deadly nightshade
Deadly nightshade, also known as belladonna, can cause hallucinations, resulting in death. The side effects that the human body may face upon the consumption of any parts, especially the berries, of the Deadly Nightshade Plant: Blurred Vision. Dry Mouth.Difficulty Swallowing.Rapid heartbeat (tachycardia). Hallucinations and Delirium. Fever. Seizures. ... Respiratory Failure.
